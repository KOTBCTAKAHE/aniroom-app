Each file mixes a cue from "UI SFX" by Romain Simon (https://uisfx.com,
https://github.com/romainsimon/uisfx), dedicated to the public domain under
CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), with a
synthesized whoosh / clock-tick / impact layer made for AniRoom.
